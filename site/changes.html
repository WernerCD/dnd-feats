Feats, species and backgrounds for 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e rules

Changes

What has changed on Feats & Origins, newest first. Back to Feats & Origins.

November 19, 2026

  • Works offline. After one visit, Feats & Origins and its full list of feats, species and backgrounds open with no signal, and it can be added to a phone's home screen with its own icon.
  • Search from the hub. Every feat, species and background is in the D&D Toolbox's "Search every tool" box, and the result opens that entry here.
  • Preview cards. Links to this site shared in chat apps show a picture and description.

November 18, 2026

  • Core books first. The Sources menu lists the core rules (SRD, Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide) at the top, ticked, with supplements, settings and partner books such as World of Warcraft grouped below and unticked but one click away. "Core books" goes back to that default; switching edition or tab keeps it.
  • Imported characters. When a character has been imported on the D&D Toolbox Characters page (from D&D Beyond, Foundry, Fight Club 5 or DiceCloud), this site offers to use its feats, species and background, shows what it will change and what it couldn't find, and changes nothing until you choose.
  • First release. Feats, species and backgrounds for the 2014 and 2024 rules in one place, with the full SRD text where the free rules have it and an index of the official books (facts and an original one-line summary) for everything else.
  • Filters you can share. Search, and filter by feat category, kind of prerequisite, the ability score an entry raises, source book and repeatable feats. The link keeps every filter, and Copy link copies it.
  • Your character. Pick a character in the header to see which feats it qualifies for (ability scores, level, class, spellcasting, armor training and feats it already has), mark feats as Taken or Planned, and set its species and background. "At level" checks prerequisites for a level you are planning.
  • Character Sheet and Party. A character's taken and planned feats, species and background now show on that character's sheet and on the Party page in the D&D Toolbox, and travel with its share link.
  • Consistent design. The header, footer, theme and change log follow the shared D&D Toolbox look.
