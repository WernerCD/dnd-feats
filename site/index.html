About

Browse feats, species (races) and backgrounds for the 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) and the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2). Entries from the free System Reference Documents show their full rules text. Entries from the other official books are listed as an index: name, book, page, category, prerequisite, ability increase and a one-line summary written for this site, so you know where to look them up.

Pick a character in the header (shared with the other D&D Toolbox tools) to see which feats it qualifies for, mark feats as taken or planned, and set its species and background so species prerequisites can be checked. Prerequisites that depend on things the character record doesn't hold (a campaign, a background feature, a tool) are marked check rather than guessed.

Text marked SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2 comes from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. It was retrieved through the Open5e API. The index of other books (facts only, no rules text) was compiled from the community 5etools dataset; the summaries are original.
